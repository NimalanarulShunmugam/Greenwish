# Greenwish

Greenwish is a product for creating personalized, interactive wish pages. The first milestone is to turn the existing birthday prototype into a reusable, data-driven birthday page creator.

## Project contents

- `GREENWISH_PLAN.md` - product idea, first-version scope, architecture, milestones, and privacy checklist
- `index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js`, `assets/` - birthday recipient-page prototype

## First development milestone

Build a flow where a customer can enter names, a personal message, quiz content, and puzzle decoys; preview and edit the page; and save it as a draft. Add payment and public share links only after the draft-to-preview experience works.

## Important

Do not store customer content, payment credentials, or secrets in this repository. Before publishing the repository, review photos and music in `assets/` and confirm you have permission to distribute them. See `GREENWISH_PLAN.md` for the full product plan.
